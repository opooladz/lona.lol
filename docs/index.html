The method

Four steps. Twelve weeks. One score that gets you in.

Every cohort runs the same proven arc. We start by figuring out exactly where you are, build a plan around your real weaknesses, train under live coaching, then condition you for test day so nothing about the morning of feels new. No filler weeks. No "study harder" advice.

01

Step 1

Diagnose for direction

We start with a full baseline diagnostic so we can see exactly where you're stuck. No guessing, no generic study plan — the first week is about getting an honest map of your current score by section.

02

Step 2

Target and train

You focus only on the topics and strategies that actually move your score. We cut everything that isn't earning you points, and we drill the high-yield content together, live, every week.

03

Step 3

Condition for confidence

You start building test-day readiness with timed drills, smart review cycles, and the mental rehearsal most students skip. By this stage you're not just learning content — you're learning how to take this specific test under pressure.

04

Step 4

Lock in readiness

You leave with a clear study plan, a mock-test rhythm, and the kind of momentum that carries you through the final stretch on your own. By test morning, nothing about the format, the timing, or the question style is unfamiliar.

Common questions

What pre-dentals usually want to know.

Still curious about something specific? The free strategy call is the right place — we'll answer the questions that are actually about your situation, not the generic ones.

Book the free call →
How much time do I really need to study?
Most students who get the score they want give the DAT 10–16 weeks of focused prep, somewhere between 15 and 25 hours a week. The 12-week cohort is built around that midpoint. If your test is sooner, we'll be honest with you on the free call about whether you should push your date.
What if I've already taken the DAT once and didn't get the score I wanted?
Retakers are about a third of who I coach. We don't start from scratch — we start from your last score report. The diagnostic tells us what's actually changed (and what didn't), and we rebuild from there. Most retakers come in already knowing the content; what we fix is the strategy, the timing, and the avoidance pattern around one specific section.
Do you guarantee a score?
Yes — within reason. If you complete the cohort (at least 80% of live sessions and at least 4 full mock exams) and your AA doesn't go up from your starting diagnostic, you get a 50% refund or a free seat in the next cohort. The guarantee exists because I want you to actually do the work, not because I think you'll need to use it.
What's the difference between you and DAT Bootcamp?
Bootcamp is a software platform — 11,000 questions, video library, no human. I'm a coach. I open your practice tests with you, I notice when you've been avoiding PAT for two weeks, and I know what your score needs to be for the schools you're applying to. Most of my students actually keep using Bootcamp's question bank alongside the cohort. The two solve different problems.
How small are the groups, really?
Cohorts cap at 8. Most run with 5–7. I'd rather under-enroll than have a group where you can hide.
What if I miss a live session?
Every session is recorded, and you'll get a written recap from me with the homework before the next one. If you have to miss two or more in a row, we'll do a quick 15-minute catch-up call so you don't fall behind.
When do new cohorts start?
I run a new cohort roughly every 8–10 weeks so there's always one starting soon. Email me or book a free call and I'll send you the next start date and lock your seat — enrollment closes as soon as the 8 spots fill.
Can I get a refund if it's not for me?
Yes. If you decide within the first two weeks that the cohort isn't a fit, I'll refund you in full — no awkward email required. After week two, the higher-score guarantee above is the version that applies.
